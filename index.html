WELCOME TO LITTLE WORLD

Five minutes to make your mark.

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move in screen directions. Combine keys for smooth diagonal movement.

Stay on the grass: trees and water will gently stop you. The view stays fixed as you explore.

Aim with your mouse. Click / 1 fires Piercing Thrust. Space / 2 dashes along your last movement direction. Q / E / 3 casts Bloom at your cursor.

Most kills after five minutes wins; ties use fewer deaths, then player ID. Bloom marks a circle for one second before dealing 45 damage. Regenerate after three seconds out of combat. Respawn after five seconds with a two-second shield.
