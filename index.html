WELCOME TO LITTLE WORLD

A gem of a skirmish.

Use WASD or the arrow keys to take a step. Hold a key to keep wandering.

Stay on the grass: trees and water will gently stop you. The view stays fixed as you explore.

Aim with your mouse. Click / 1 fires Pulse. Space / 2 dashes along your last movement direction. Q / E / 3 casts Bloom at your cursor.

Collect gems at the center mine. Hold 10 as a team for 15 seconds to win. Falling in combat drops all your gems. Solo rooms include a practice dummy.
